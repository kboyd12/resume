# Kaleb Boyd

Kaleb Boyd - A Geospatial Engineer and Data Scientist with with extensive experience in intelligence analysis, advanced analytics, and cybersecurity across the Defense and Private Sector.
He completed an M.S. in Analytics with a focus in Computational Data Analytics from Georgia Institute of Technology and holds a B.S. in Civil Engineering from the University of Georgia.
His current work focuses on developing advanced target and custody fusion engine systems capable of processing thousands of observations per second from multiple disparate intelligence sources across classified networks.
His work supports scalable solutions using Kafka for data ingestion, FastAPI for API services, and Kubernetes for deployment management, providing contextual and spatio-temporal correlations to enhance intelligence 
analysis workflows for Air Force 480th ISR Wing, Air Force Combat Command, and Air Force Research Laboratory customers.
His prior work includes developing custom Large Language Model (LLM) agentic workflows, along with serving as a GEOINT Analyst at the United States Navy, where he pioneered the integration of data science in naval operations.
